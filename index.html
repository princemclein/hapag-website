Our Story

At HAPAG, we believe Filipino food is more than a meal—it is a way of bringing people together. Inspired by the flavors we grew up with and the traditions passed down through generations, we create dishes that celebrate the heart of Filipino cooking. From familiar favorites to reimagined classics, every plate is made to be shared, remembered, and enjoyed together.

Togetherness
